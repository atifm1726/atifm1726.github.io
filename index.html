I am a Database Administrator with a strong analytics layer on top. My day-to-day is keeping data systems reliable: tuning queries, designing schemas, managing backups, and making sure production databases never become the bottleneck. At NayaPay (Lakson Group of Companies), I administered the databases behind a fintech platform processing millions of daily transactions, then used that same access to build the ETL pipelines and Power BI dashboards that turned the data into business intelligence for senior leadership.

I bring a rare combination: the engineering discipline of a DBA who treats data integrity as non-negotiable, and the curiosity of an analyst who refuses to stop at the dashboard. My MS capstone at the University of Illinois Springfield extended that range into machine learning, producing a multi-label chest disease detection model that exceeded the MedMNIST v2 ResNet benchmarks with a mean AUC of 0.7794.
